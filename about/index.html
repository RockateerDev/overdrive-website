About

Overdrive Games exists with the goal to preserve, charge and reignite the passion of '80s culture through the immersive medium of games.

Striving to combine elements from the cinema, music, and aesthetics of the 1980s decade with the heat and spirit of neo-retrofuturism movements found on the internet today. Old and new collide to capture both the grit and grandeur of one of the most legendary periods in human culture.

Based in the heart of Guildford, UK - operated as a proudly independent solo project.
